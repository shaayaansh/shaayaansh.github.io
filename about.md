My work spans natural language processing, multimodal systems, and representation learning. I'm particularly drawn to problems where structure matters: aligning sequences, modeling latent signals, understanding robustness, and designing systems that behave reliably under distribution shifts.

I enjoy thinking about the mechanics behind learning systems - how objectives shape behavior, how representations encode bias, and how small modeling decisions scale into large effects.
